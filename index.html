How It Works

Both inputs are drawn with the same total area. The generator finds connected pieces that fit in each arrangement, allowing them to move and rotate. Every square is used once in each arrangement.

Spaces are preserved. Longer phrases can wrap at a word boundary. Use Board size to enlarge a puzzle without changing its pieces.

The puzzle is generated in your browser. Shared links contain the entered text, colors, and a seed so another browser can recreate the same puzzle.
